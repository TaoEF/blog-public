# 2026-W21 Source Notes

## Selected Story

Peacock updated its streaming-service logo in May 2026 with refined type and one
fewer color dot. This is the best first story because it combines a famous brand,
a clear logo-design change, streaming-industry relevance, and practical lessons
for digital-first identity systems.

## Editorial Position

This is less a full rebrand than a digital-product refinement. The important
lesson is that a mature streaming logo does not always need a new concept; it
often needs better legibility, icon behavior, and system consistency.
